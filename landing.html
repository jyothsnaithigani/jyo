Frequently Asked Questions

How does your pricing and packages work?

Every job starts with a free consultation, so pricing is based on your actual scope rather than a fixed catalogue rate. Offline work is quoted by size, material and quantity; online work can be booked as a monthly package or a one-time project. There's no fixed minimum budget.

What's the difference between offline and online advertising with you?

Offline advertising covers hoardings and banners, flex and vinyl printing, and pamphlets and flyers. Online advertising covers SEO, social media marketing and website design. We can run either on its own or combine both under one plan.

How long does print or outdoor work take?

Most hoarding, flex and pamphlet jobs move quickly once artwork is approved. Exact turnaround depends on size, material and placement, so we confirm a timeline for your specific job before you book it in.

How do you measure results for digital campaigns?

For SEO and social media we track rankings, website traffic and engagement over time. For digital ad campaigns we track clicks, enquiries and cost per lead, and share these numbers with you.

Is there a minimum contract length?

No long lock-ins by default. Offline jobs are typically one-off or project-based, and digital packages run month to month, so you can scale up, pause, or adjust as your needs change.

How often will I get reports or updates?

Digital clients get regular monthly reporting on performance, with your account contact available in between for questions. Offline jobs are updated at each stage, from artwork approval to installation.

Which areas of Andhra Pradesh & Telangana do you cover?

We cover all of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, including major cities such as Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Warangal and Guntur, as well as surrounding towns.
