How Pricing & Packages Work

  • Every job starts with a free consultation, so pricing is based on your actual scope — not a fixed catalogue rate.
  • Offline work (hoardings, flex, pamphlets) is quoted by size, material and quantity, with the cost confirmed before we print.
  • Online work (SEO, social, website, ads) can be booked as a monthly package or a one-time project, depending on your goals.
  • Combine offline and online services and we'll quote them together — no hidden charges added later.
  • There's no fixed minimum budget; we build a plan around what you have to spend.
